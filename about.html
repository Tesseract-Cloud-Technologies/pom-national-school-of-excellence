About POMNATHS

Port Moresby National High School — the National School of Excellence.

History

Opened in 1996 on Goro-Kaeaga Road, Gerehu, POMNATHS has grown into one of Papua New Guinea's leading secondary schools. In 2020 it became the national STEM flagship school.
